OUR CORE MISSION

To eliminate AI hallucinations by anchoring generative intelligence in cited, verifiable human documents.

In an era of conversational chatbots that speak with unwarranted confidence yet frequently fabricate facts, Knowledge Studio was conceived with a strict standard: truth must be mathematically verifiable. Every paragraph produced is grounded in explicit chunk passages from your trusted files.

Developed within the Department of Computer Science at COMSATS University Islamabad, Knowledge Studio originated as an advanced capstone inquiry into Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Confronted with the constant student dilemma of parsing hundreds of textbook chapters, dynamic web pages, and recorded seminar videos, our team engineered a unified RAG platform that bridges disparate data silos into a singular conversational interface.

From dense vector chunking and cosine similarity thresholds to our signature cream-and-quiet visual ergonomics, the system reflects both academic rigor and thoughtful software craft.

FOUNDATION & ARCHITECTURE

Engineered for Truth & Clarity.

Three core tenets define the Knowledge Studio engine, ensuring every retrieved answer is factual, multi-source, and cognitively calming.

Zero-Hallucination Grounding

Generative language models are bounded by deterministic cosine similarity thresholds (≥ 0.78), citing explicit chunk passages directly from your files.

✓ Strict cosine cutoffs (≥ 0.78)
✓ Inline clickable chunk citations

Modular Data Ingestion (PDF, Web, YT)

A unified tripartite data adapter that ingests diverse mediums: high-precision PDF parsing, DOM web crawling, and timestamped YouTube transcripts.

✓ Tables, figures & structured PDFs
✓ Millisecond video timestamp alignment

Designed to mitigate screen fatigue during prolonged academic research. Soft cream canvas (#faf8f2 / #fdfbf7) paired with soothing watercolor accents.
